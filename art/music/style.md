The music of *Stratagem* draws on the grand American symphonic tradition of mid-to-late twentieth-century cinema: the sweeping frontier landscapes of Jerome Moross (*The Big Country*) and Elmer Bernstein (*The Magnificent Seven*), the open, pastoral harmonies of Aaron Copland, the majestic exploration themes of Jerry Goldsmith (*Star Trek: The Motion Picture*), and above all the soaring sense of wonder in John Williams's *Jurassic Park*. Rich orchestration, expansive melodies, noble brass, lyrical strings and delicate woodwinds evoke magnificence, discovery, adventure and the sublime beauty of the natural world.

The *Stratagem Overture* establishes this musical identity: beginning with a sense of quiet anticipation, it opens into a magnificent panorama of mountains, forests, rivers and the ancient geological world beneath them. Its themes express both the exhilaration of exploration and the mystery of the unknown, tempered by the vulnerability of a lone traveller on the frontier. Above all, the music celebrates **the world rather than the player**. Nature is beautiful, immense, ancient and ultimately indifferent to human ambitions. *Stratagem* is not about conquering that world, but about discovering, understanding and learning to live within it.
